# Third-party notices and license boundaries

The project LICENSE applies only to original project contributions. This notice is not a blanket relicensing of third-party files. Resource-by-resource roles, authoritative citation metadata and versions/commits are in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md) and `data/source_index/UPSTREAM_RESOURCES.json`.

Scientific wheels contain transitive libraries, including OpenBLAS/LAPACK and GCC runtime exceptions; their original bundled notices are retained where present. These are dependency terms, not a claim that our original code is governed by all wheel licenses. Preferred scholarly citation does not replace license/attribution obligations. For unbundled corpora and packages obtain upstream distributions and follow their actual terms; our bibliography is not a license grant.

`licenses/upstream/` preserves original supplied license/citation/NOTICE documents, including disputed/incorrect inherited metadata. The new audit explains corrections; it never edits the original records. Original author aliases appearing in third-party preferred citations (for example pypdf) are retained as upstream metadata. **Dylan Bedford** is the primary author of this contribution.
