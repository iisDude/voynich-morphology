# Limits of the evidence

- **Single-AI source adjudication.** Writing membership, whole-parent extent and same/partial/different judgments come from one AI adjudicator. Anonymous repeats measure same-adjudicator repeatability, not independent inter-rater accuracy. No independent palaeographer participated. Prior visual memory and short repeat-session separation remain.
- **Previous image exposure.** Fresh captions were fresh relative to audited detailed fitting/source/pair uses, not globally unseen photographs. Earlier overview exposure and broad V4 processing existed. Caption grouping is not independently certified bifolio, panel, hand or section identity.
- **Proposal-conditioned sampling.** Selected ordinary fields and sampled connected-parent proposals are not a manuscript census or pixel-exhaustive independent writing sample. Detector omissions, quality selection, cropping and repeated parents can bias results.
- **Whole-parent uncertainty.** Unknown physical extents remain unknown. Resolved source extent does not imply a pixel-perfect physical contour. Finite threshold/slant/split/merge candidates cannot bound all photographic or physical possibilities. Envelopes are sensitivity ranges, not posterior probabilities.
- **Rating instability.** Partial/different discrimination is weak relative to same/different discrimination. In the larger repeat set exact agreement is 80.8%; category transitions remain. Conditioning on stable resolved ratings would overstate agreement.
- **Repeated-parent and caption dependence.** Original pairs reuse parents. Parent-disjoint and dyad-limited subsets are smaller and sometimes underpowered. Caption resampling is conditional on eight captions and does not cover systematic adjudication bias. Degenerate small-subset intervals do not imply perfect population accuracy.
- **Sampling-mixture sensitivity.** Broad-random and aspect-matched results differ. Uniform partial recoding changes the task and denominators; the two reported recodings are not sharp bounds over all individual reallocations.
- **Source/recovery confounds.** Image contrast, raster threshold, parent/body scale, crop context and neighboring ink affect geometry. Morphology associations with caption would remain confounded with photograph and sampling quality. Whole shapes can be similar for unrelated reasons.
- **Failed sequence support.** V4/V5 cannot certify complete ordinary rows or general complete structural sequences. Ignoring optional detections inflates completeness. V6 added no validated classes. Partial feature trials failed their intended gates. Later morphology success does not erase these negatives.
- **Reproduction scope.** Fixed annotations are reproducible records, not regenerable source truth. Native source re-extraction requires independently acquired, hash-matching images. Floating-point bitwise replay was checked with the recorded Windows scientific runtime; other BLAS/platform builds can differ. The original PDF export cannot be assumed identical to a newly downloaded export. Full source-image re-acquisition was not repeated during packaging.
- **No writing-system interpretation.** There is no claim of letters, graphemes, characters, allographs, words, language, pen lifts, physical stroke order, semantic identity, decipherment or complete transcription. Neighbor sets are not equivalence classes. Conventional transcriptions are historical comparisons, not construction inputs.
