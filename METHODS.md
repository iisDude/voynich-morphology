# Methodological development

The primary evidence is the photographed manuscript. The initial handoff required physical page, row and ink-group extraction before conventional comparison. PDF-derived views were used for calibration; native Yale IIIF photographs subsequently provided the principal adjudication evidence. Conventional files were excluded from representation construction, though the investigator had prior exposure to historical project results. This is procedural exclusion, not global investigator blindness.

## Ground-up source and segmentation

The twelve-view pilot calibrated layout, stroke/connectivity, height/body references, tall structures, bench/frame structures, possible insertions/compounds, repetition, curved writing and panel mapping. Connected raster regions were proposals, never automatically letters. Several extractions and candidate alphabets failed. Source-adjudication queues prioritized consequential uncertain writing/drawing membership, contacts, detached regions, field edges and ownership. V2 preserved unknowns and competing segmentations; the historical Currier-B positional assay after that freeze was not estimable because qualifying minimal-pair support was absent. Native x and normalized group rank are distinct measurements. No such conventional assay is reopened here.

## Recurrent structural classes: V3

Broader ordinary-field extraction separated incomplete source coverage from over-specific shape classification. The first fine contour/topology classifier failed agreement/stability criteria and over-separated visibly related forms. A separate source-reviewed structural competitor was specified and evaluated using reserved caption groups, not conventional labels or sequence success. Fifteen hypotheses yielded fourteen supported recurrent classes. ST labels describe whole visible structural organization; they do not identify graphemes, components drawn separately, stroke order or equivalence of meaning. Source extent and raster-assignment abstention remain distinct. The original candidate failure, protocol, pair judgments and models are all retained.

## Sequence and source membership: V4 and V5

V4 froze V3 unchanged, expanded source/layout-selected fields and ordered candidate parents by native x. Boundary hypotheses at 0.35/0.55/0.75 body-proxy gaps remained separate. Optional faint/detached regions, ownership alternatives, connected structures crossing gaps and edge truncation prevented certification of complete row membership. Conditional short repeated lists did not satisfy the practical-notation gates.

V5 stopped breadth expansion. Sixteen ordinary row targets from eight captions received source object-location adjudication before class mapping. The reference keeps confirmed writing, nonwriting, unresolved and mixed cases, native coordinates, source endpoint intervals and optional members. Source-only extraction rules were developed on development rows and tested on held-out rows. Optional detector regions were investigated rather than discarded. Same-adjudicator anonymous repeat judgments caused resolved-to-unknown downgrades before freezing. Endpoints and detected white-space gaps were observable; semantic group boundaries remained unknown. Neither V4 nor V5 supports full transcription or complete long structural sequences.

## Unknown parents: V6

Only source-confirmed V5 writing entered unknown-parent discovery. Failure causes included V3-family variation, raster/topology sensitivity, compounds/contacts, appendices and source ambiguity. Composite/factor descriptions were considered before multiplying classes. Fresh caption source review and fixed-radius tests did not validate a new recurrent class. The absence of accepted classes is preserved, not repaired by loosening parameters. V3 and V5 membership were unchanged.

## Neutral feature trials

Trial 1 tested 34 neutral geometry/distribution/cavity/connectivity/run/graph descriptors with uncertainty states. Partial profiles were feasible, but validation coverage failed. Height/body and width/body tolerances were intrinsically smaller than the normalization perturbation range; this methodological defect is explicitly preserved in Trial 1.

Trial 2 preregistered revisions before its outcomes: 36 descriptors, 25 core, uncertain graph fields and a compatible dimensional tolerance. Source-visible cavities required source qualification; a repeatable raster hole did not certify a source cavity. Fresh-caption coverage and V3-unknown coverage remained below the intended support thresholds. The feature representation was not tuned using class labels, sequence recurrence or linguistic outcomes.

## Direct continuous morphology

Whole-parent source evidence remains primary; unresolved whole extent has no full physical embedding. Each observed recovery is tightly bounded, aspect-preserved, centered with a 4-pixel margin on each side in a 64×64 binary field (56-pixel support), with no rotation/reflection alignment. Signed-distance fields are a secondary representation. The primary contour64 metric is symmetric mean contour Chamfer distance, using the frozen OpenCV DIST_L2 mask3 raster approximation and 56-pixel normalization. The 32/128 contour fields are sensitivity checks; SDF is secondary. No PCA, clustering, learned embedding, class prototype or complete hand-designed feature vector is part of this representation.

Candidate ensembles retain primary contrast9, matched contrast6/12 recoveries, split fragments/unions, merges/contamination hypotheses and synthetic ±0.04 slant sensitivities according to the unchanged Trial 1 code. A threshold match does not settle physical parent extent. The minimum/maximum observed pair distance is an observed sensitivity envelope, not a probability, confidence interval or bound over all physically possible shapes. No raster alternative is selected for obtaining a favorable match.

Trial 1 reused exposed similarity judgments and establishes feasibility only. Trial 2 audited prior caption use, sealed selection, inspected native RGB, froze source judgments, applied the unchanged representation and sealed deterministic cross-caption broad-random/aspect-matched sampling before anonymous source similarity ratings. Same/partial/different/unresolved judgments remained distinct. A preregistered extension from 240 to 360 judgments was triggered by inadequate same support; the unused pool remained unopened. Binary AUROC excluded partial/unresolved. Registered sample, contour64, aspect advantage, caption-node bootstrap, replay and unknown-preservation gates were frozen before prediction evaluation. Source overlap with earlier overview work is explicitly recorded.

## Robustness

The separately frozen post-trial robustness protocol tested parent-disjoint, caption-dyad-limited and combined subsets using maximum-cardinality selection with seeded priorities, without selecting on metric or category balance. Source-extent and raster-recovery filters were separate. Extent-clean filtering excluded nothing from the originally qualified pair pool; recovery filtering did. All uniform partial recodings and adjacent category contrasts were reported; neither uniform recoding is adjudicated truth or a sharp bound over mixed allocations. Broad-random and aspect-matched strata were separate. A 125-pair anonymous repeat set reported exact, ordinal-weighted and category-specific same-adjudicator agreement. Five-thousand caption-node resamples and deterministic selection sensitivity conditioned on this graph/sample; they cannot remove source/rater bias.

The robustness classification is sensitive to one or more evaluation assumptions. The original supported Trial 2 status and every earlier freeze remain unchanged. This snapshot performs numerical replay and packaging, not a new validation, revised segmentation or linguistic investigation.
