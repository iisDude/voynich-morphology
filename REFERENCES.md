## Corrections, missing metadata and exclusions

The inherited NOTICE misidentifies Davis’s 2020 work; the verified title is *How Many Glyphs and How Many Scribes?* (DOI 10.1353/mns.2020.0011). A preliminary Crossref lookup at 10.1353/mns.2020.0009 and preliminary ACL lookups C16-1134/L08-1507 returned unrelated works and were rejected. The correct Turkish preferred paper is C16-1325; PROIEL authors’ bibliography establishes the 2008 workshop paper. Original inherited documents and freeze manifests were not edited.

The broad inherited bibliography (including unimplemented cipher/generator hypotheses) is not automatically a list of resources used here. Downloaded repository code was not executed; static AST comparison found no exact matches of nontrivial functions, which cannot prove absence of conceptual influence. All five supplied archive hashes and ZIP Git comments are preserved in the audit. Historical UD corpora and conventional text source files remain external, with supplied hashes and acquisition information. RF is derived from ZL/GC, so its agreement is dependent.

Transitive Python packages are enumerated in `environment/requirements-lock.txt` and the observed lock. Numerical wheels can incorporate OpenBLAS, LAPACK, GCC runtimes and other libraries; original wheel notices are preserved. No wheels/binaries are redistributed. Codex AI assisted source adjudication and implementation; it is not listed as an author or independent human rater. No independent palaeographer participated.
