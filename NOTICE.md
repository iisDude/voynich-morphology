# Source-image attribution and reuse

Voynich manuscript imagery: **Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Yale University, Beinecke MS408 (Cipher manuscript)**. Collection record: https://collections.library.yale.edu/catalog/2002046. Exact image IDs, full-resolution retrieval URLs, dimensions, native crop coordinates and original byte hashes are retained in `data/source_index/` and each source-parent table.

Yale's [2011 policy on open access to digital representations of public-domain works](https://web.library.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/policy_-_open_access_to_digital_representations_of_works_in_the_public_domain_from_museum_library_and_archive_collections_2011_.pdf) provides unrestricted access/reuse for digital copies of public-domain collection works. See also the [Yale Library reuse policy](https://www.library.yale.edu/policies/reuse) and [Beinecke manuscript description](https://beinecke.library.yale.edu/beinecke/collections/beinecke-cipher-voynich-manuscript). Attribution does not imply Yale endorsement or grant rights to Yale trademarks. The project does not claim ownership of the medieval manuscript or Yale source-image pixels.

Full native source JPEGs and the full manuscript PDF are not bundled. Attributed source-derived RGB crops, annotated examples and raster-derived morphology are included as inspection evidence. Project annotation/layout/numerical contributions are separately licensed; the source pixels retain their source/public-domain status. Original file-level notices and supplied upstream license texts remain separate from project licensing. Yale item-catalog automated retrieval returned an anti-bot response during this audit; the institutional reuse policy and manuscript description were separately verified. A permissive institutional policy is not an assertion that the project created the source imagery.
