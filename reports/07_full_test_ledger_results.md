# Test ledger disposition

Every entry below points to saved code, configurations, source hashes, machine-readable results, uncertainty or non-estimability, and readable summaries. Exact undocumented historical scripts were not recovered. “Analogue” means a newly explicit implementation of the stated question, not an exact reproduction of an old chat number. Failures are retained. Existing transcriptions entered only after the original visual freeze; the tracked v11 repair is explicitly post-exposure.

The common primary pair specification is frequency ≥8, two-unit edges and encoded length ≥5. OLS controls length and log geometric-mean pair frequency; HC3, connected-family clustering when at least ten families, joint caption/document occurrence bootstrap, leave-one-caption deletion and within-line slot nulls are separate outputs. Source group rank and measured native pixel x are separate endpoints. Ordinary-language controls have sentence ordinal rank only.

## Coverage of the ground-up workflow

Physical imagery, rows/groups, stroke-mask connectivity, baseline/height proxies, tall structures, frames/possible insertions, ligature/compound alternatives, repetitions, unresolved pen lifts, visual recurrence, competing partitions, contextual variation, hand/section stability, a neutral atlas and frozen source-derived snapshots all have saved artifacts. The original visual freeze preceded comparison parsing and assays. Whole-row source audits and crosswalk rejection prevent promoting these snapshots to a certified complete unit system.

The tracked snapshot is a source-only repair after exposure, tested as a separate version. Its frozen prototypes/gates are not retuned to recover conventional effects. Circular/sparse writing, complete panel/bifolio mapping, drawing exclusion, faint/detached ink and contact ownership remain unvalidated. These are evidence limitations, not proof that no writing structure exists.

## Reproduction limits

The supplied historical numerical outputs are provenance. This pass preserves meaningful differences caused by explicit parsing, encoded boundaries, length rules, block definitions and leakage control; it does not force agreement with chat numbers. No plaintext, meanings, historically established writing direction, alphabet size, computational runtime or authorship count follows from these tests.
