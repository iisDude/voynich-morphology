# Source recurrence: bottlenecks and structural model V3

V2 remains immutable. Recurring **connected parent structures** are supported by held-out manuscript imagery; sparse **complete assembled group sequences** are a different problem. Missing or uncertain source membership, conjunctive admission of every parent in a longer list, overly fine visual labels, and competing boundaries all contribute. This investigation quantifies observable exclusions and matched comparisons; it cannot assign a causal percentage of unseen manuscript writing to each cause.

The source-only structural candidate passes the registered point-estimate gates and qualifies for V3 freezing. It has 15 structural class hypotheses, **14 with corroborated recurrence on seven fresh test caption groups**. One experimental class and unresolved instances remain unknown. This is a local parent segmentation and structural taxonomy, not a recovered alphabet or certified word segmentation. No positional assay was rerun.

## Separate coverage and classification contributions

The immutable V2 source-only group table contains 4,136 proposed groups. Its disjoint gate accounting is:

| Stage | Groups lost or retained | Share of original proposals |
|---|---:|---:|
| Source membership/boundary unresolved | 2,655 lost | 64.2% |
| Old visual-family gate, after source acceptance | 753 lost | 18.2% |
| Whole-row extent gate, after local fine completion | 563 lost | 13.6% |
| Fully retained by those gates | 165 retained | 4.0% |

The whole-row gate removes 77.3% of the 728 locally complete old fine sequences. That is an extent-certification loss, not a failure of local form recurrence. V2 already required source families to recur during training; the sparse downstream object was the conjunction of family labels and boundaries across an entire group.

On the **same 728 occurrences**, repeated-sequence coverage is 36.4% with fine labels, 64.6% with V2 broad labels, and 67.4% with its factor hypotheses. The 28.2 percentage-point fine-to-broad difference isolates label specificity on fixed source membership. Those earlier merges are diagnostic comparisons, not the basis of V3 class definitions or evidence that their accuracy was validated.

The new source-atlas comparison holds pixels, admission, nearest training exemplars and candidate boundaries fixed. For 237 retained lists with at least three parents under the fixed 0.55-body-gap hypothesis, recurrence rises from **19.4% with contour subclasses to 25.3% with structural classes**: a 5.9-point specificity contribution. These are exact list repeats, not minimal pairs or words.

Separately, source-caption subsampling without replacement holds the taxonomy fixed:

| Caption groups sampled | Mean retained lists, ≥3 parents | Contour-subclass recurrence | Structural-class recurrence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 24.0 | 5.4% | 7.1% |
| 6 | 48.2 | 7.7% | 10.8% |
| 12 | 94.2 | 10.7% | 15.1% |
| 30 | 237.0 | 19.4% | 25.3% |

Coverage growth contributes 14.0 points with the contour taxonomy and 18.2 with the structural taxonomy. Grouping contributes about 1.7 points at three captions and 5.9 at thirty. The effects interact; they should not be added into a manuscript-wide causal attribution. This is finite-sample coverage of reviewed fields, not estimated recall of all writing.

![Observed gate accounting and matched source comparisons](../figures/recurrence_v3/bottleneck_accounting.png)

Boundary hypotheses also matter. Among lists with at least three parents, structural recurrence is 36.4%, 25.3%, and 19.4% for gaps of 0.35, 0.55, and 0.75 body heights respectively. Their eligible list counts differ. No gap was selected to maximize recurrence or recover an earlier effect; all remain competing source hypotheses.

## Expanded source evidence and remaining unknowns

Across the two candidate stages, **61 captures and 1,184 ordinary-row proposals** received source-field review. The final structural corpus contains 49 captures, 918 proposals, 899 retained local writing fields, and **20,037 unique proposed connected parents**. Of these, 15,146 have stable significant-hole topology, connectivity and sufficient overlap across contrast 6/9/12. A stricter corroboration step retains structural labels for 10,712 parents; **9,325 remain unknown**. Atomicity and pen lifts remain unknown for every parent.

This is substantially broader ordinary-row coverage than V2's 390 proposed reviewed group rows, but these counts are not certified independent physical lines. Overlapping detector windows can describe the same writing. Native-view connected-parent IDs deduplicate those windows; row ownership can remain unknown while a local parent shape is available for comparison.

The prioritized field queue excluded painted leaves/flowers, berry and stem outlines, bathing figures, bowls, marginal stars/tails, binding/page-number areas, and several empty/drawing proposals. It retained conservative clear writing intervals rather than clipping drawings into pseudo-units. The wide V_179 capture remains unknown because its overview does not decide native panel membership/orientation. V_158 and V_154 use separate local fields; no new physical panel identity or reading order is asserted. Curved, vertical, rotated and radial writing remains outside the ordinary-row confirmation corpus and retains the earlier unresolved evidence.

Field review and fine contact adjudication are different levels of evidence. Ten initial captures received full row-context-sheet inspection; the remaining locations received native-source overview field triage. Native-pixel extraction, threshold comparisons, training exemplars and sampled native RGB pairs supply finer checks. **918 proposals were not exhaustively adjudicated as complete physical rows.** Highest-resolution Yale captures already verified in the project supplied all measurements; display magnification added no source detail.

At the 0.55 gap, 617 of 4,190 candidate lists have a complete retained parent list. The disjoint remaining exclusions are 2,448 unstable/unresolved parent rasters, 142 competing row ownership cases, 923 unknown structural classes or perturbation disagreements, and 60 local-field-edge cases. Even the 617 lists can omit small detached ink; “complete retained list” does not certify a complete physical group. Longer lists require more uncertain parents to pass simultaneously. This explains why reusable forms can be abundant while complete sequence evidence remains scarce.

## Source-only class criteria and competing models

The first candidate used contour proximity, relative geometry and topology-constrained K=16/24/32/48/64 clustering. Train/validation selection chose K=64; significant-hole subdivisions yielded 178 candidate classes. It was sealed before its test class assignments were inspected. It failed: threshold assignment stability was 79.1% against an 80% requirement, and native positive-pair agreement was 82.5% against 85%. Moreover, 27/40 proposed other-class hard negatives were judged to have the same structural organization. Fine partitions were separating width, slant, density and proportional variants. This failed candidate remains saved.

A new reserve was then registered: V_045, V_030, V_102, V_089, V_154, V_120 and V_202, seven caption groups absent from the first candidate corpus. The broader taxonomy was defined from a **training-only 16-cluster source atlas**, with eight examples from distinct captions per cluster. Individual merges did not follow the earlier test pairs.

The source criteria distinguish paired uprights with a bridge, horizontal arch bridges, an upper curve with a lower terminal, vertical loop pairs, compact rings, compact loops with an upper/right trace, loop pairs with a left appendix, a single upright with lateral bridge, compact open hooks, short oblique terminals, and loops with oblique lower branches. Width, slant, density and small proportional variation may be nuisance dimensions. An extra upright, bridge, connected branch or lower appendix preserves a competing class. Open/closed topology variants remain separate. A mixed training cluster containing stacked/inter-row constructions was rejected as a universal class.

Whole connected parents remain primary. Detached attachment, ligature/compound identity, stroke order, pen lifts and internal grapheme cuts are not inferred from a photograph. Significant holes and skeleton descriptors are raster measurements, not assumed pen strokes. Upper/lower factor signatures describe relative geometry rather than letters or an internal alphabet.

No EVA/RF/v101 labels, Currier metadata, inherited hand/section labels, positional outcomes, or minimal-pair existence chose classes, merges, thresholds or boundaries. Earlier handoff findings had already been disclosed, so researcher blinding is incomplete. The fresh reserve is independent of the first candidate's class selection by caption provenance; it is not a newly blind historical experiment or certified bifolio split. Original comparison files were integrity-hashed only in this continuation.

## Held-out validation and freeze qualification

The second model and configuration were sealed before fresh-test class suggestions were inspected. No fit, radius or merge changed afterward. Case-level source disagreements become unknown rather than being relabeled to a convenient alternative.

| Registered criterion | Fresh held-out result |
|---|---:|
| At least 6 test caption groups | 7 |
| At least 10 recurring structural core classes | 14 |
| Each core: ≥3 training captions; ≥5 test instances; ≥3 test captions | All 14 pass after corroboration |
| Native positive-pair agreement ≥85% | 35/40 = 87.5% |
| Source-judged different-pair false acceptance ≤15% | 3/27 = 11.1% |
| Threshold class-assignment stability ≥80% | 93.6% before corroboration filtering |
| Clear test-parent assignment coverage ≥50% | 67.2% nominal; **60.4% corroborated** |
| Deterministic re-execution | Identical test classes and abstentions |

The source pair gallery hid proposed same/different class labels. Five pair judgments remain unknown. A classifier-different nearest neighbor is only a *proposed* hard negative; it cannot supply its own ground truth. Source-judged different pairs supply the negative denominator. Thirteen proposed negatives were judged structurally alike, exposing remaining false separation; three proposed positives were judged different, exposing false grouping. All are retained in the audit.

A separate twelve-factor nonnegative model was trained on raw parent masks. Held-out factor signatures agree better with their training structural class than with other-class controls matched on significant holes and log-aspect. Equal-caption mean error improvement is 0.957, with a caption-bootstrap interval of 0.917–0.995. A related contour control also favors assigned source exemplars. These checks support shape organization; they are not linguistic evidence or independent confirmation of an alphabet. Contour matching is related to the fitted representation, so its lower distance is not an independent accuracy claim.

There is one source adjudicator. Caption-bootstrap intervals are broad: positive agreement approximately 78.2%–100%; source-negative false acceptance 0%–26.7%. The registered **point-estimate** gates pass; precision and independent-rater reproducibility remain limited. Caption provenance is not certified physical-bifolio independence. Nonrandom ordinary-field selection limits manuscript-wide inference.

The freeze preserves the failed candidate, the 15-class taxonomy, 14 corroborated core classes, unknown parents, all three boundary hypotheses, source coordinates, model seals, negative/positive source judgments and qualification limits. The neutral atlas is [available here](12_neutral_structural_atlas_v3.html). The [model contract](../data/observations/visual_dataset_v3/model_contract.json) states the scope, and the [freeze manifest](../data/observations/visual_dataset_v3/FREEZE_MANIFEST.json) identifies immutable inputs. Each older freeze and all fourteen original evidence files were rehashed before this freeze.

Downstream assays remain deferred. Reusable parent structures have earned a local recurrence model, but incomplete physical group/line coverage and unresolved compound boundaries still prevent treating these lists as a canonical writing system or automatically reopening a positional test.
