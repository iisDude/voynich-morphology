## First annotation budget and stopping rule

For each discovery view, review up to four straight physical text paths spread across visible text regions: first, roughly one-third, roughly two-thirds, and last eligible path. Select by geometric order before looking for attractive shapes; record unavailable regions. Review up to four clear adjacent groups per path, keeping ambiguous gaps as alternatives. This gives an initial maximum of 24 paths and 96 group candidates, rather than thousands of unauditable assignments.

For circular layouts, also review one path segment and up to four isolated label groups as a separate layout subset. Do not include these in the initial horizontal-position assay. Later apply the same protocol to reserve views after freezing candidate definitions.

Flag tall extents, horizontal connectors, repeated low strokes, shared traces, and candidate compound structures wherever they occur in the selected paths. Do not choose groups because they resemble a familiar EVA form or because they support a frame hypothesis. A supplementary targeted atlas may deliberately seek rare shapes, but must label that sampling bias and exclude its frequencies from representative estimates.

Cross-page recurrence is descriptive at this stage. An assembly proposed from one view needs visually comparable instances on at least two other discovery views, or an explicit single-view/rare flag. Failure to recur is retained. A three-view criterion is a pilot evidence rule, not a declaration of unit status.

## What the pilot can resolve

The pilot is large enough to exercise reversible cropping, compare obvious shape recurrence across several layouts, and determine whether height/connectivity annotations are feasible at this resolution. It can suggest competing segmentation models and reveal a need for better scans.

It is insufficient to recover a whole-script inventory, establish hand invariance, demonstrate language/notation status, or power the reported Currier-B minimal-pair regression. Those require an expanded, registered sample with enough independent families and folios. Statistical power must be estimated from a frozen pilot before expansion; it must not be chosen to reproduce the reported coefficient.

## Blinding limits and reserve integrity

The workflow withholds transcription content from annotation. The agent has read earlier chats and knows some familiar forms and claims. Neutral aliases and reserve pages reduce procedural leakage; they do not erase prior exposure.

Panel identities, duplicated exposures, and conventional metadata coverage must be audited before using this as a formal held-out split. If discovery and reserve share a physical folio or the layout coverage is inadequate, revise the reserve assignment before annotation freezes and record the reason. Reserve membership is never decided from positional-assay performance.
