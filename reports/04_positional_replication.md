# Positional and structural replication

The positive Currier-B edge/interior contrast survives the declared primary specification in ZL, RF and v101. It is an effect in inherited encodings and normalized ordinal group rank. The source-derived candidate models do not provide a valid confirmation: original representations have no qualifying primary pairs, revised factored candidates have too few, and complete writing rows/groups remain unvalidated.

## Exact estimand and inputs

Groups are not assumed words. A pair changes one encoded unit at equal length. Primary frequency ≥8, two-unit edge, encoded length ≥5; OLS models absolute difference in mean group position with edge status, length and log geometric-mean pair frequency. True interior positions require the length restriction. Frequency 5/10/12, one-unit edges and legacy short forms are retained sensitivities.

Ordinal position is `i/(n−1)` among all original row slots (singleton 0.5). Visual pixel endpoints separately use assigned-mask group bbox center: absolute native x, native-page-normalized x, and x relative to the proposed row ink extent. Unknown visual identities do not renumber the original slots. Conventional native pixel coordinates are unknown because the source crosswalk did not certify them. Natural-language controls use UD sentence ordinal rank, not manuscript pixel coordinates.

Source SHA-256 digests, seed `20261005`, inclusion rules, all pairs, models and nulls are in `tests/structural/<dataset>`. The original visual freeze precedes inherited comparisons; v11 is a separately frozen post-exposure diagnostic repair. RF is derived from ZL/GC, not independent physical evidence.

## Dependence and boundary sensitivity

The ZL B point estimate remains +0.04784 under inherited-bifolio and quire resampling; intervals [0.02418, 0.06644] and [0.01849, 0.06227]. These blocks are inherited proxies, not a reconstructed physical binding. Nine inherited bifolio blocks cross the original train/validation/test split; graph sensitivities purge every inherited test bifolio from training, without pretending to refit the original image model on a new blind split.

Joining uncertain comma boundaries gives ZL B +0.04367 [0.02251, 0.06300]. Excluding all rows containing uncertain spaces leaves 197 pairs and +0.02664 [−0.00094, 0.05072]. The corresponding v101 exclusion gives +0.01115 [−0.01101, 0.04133]. RF retains +0.03666 [0.00996, 0.05860]. The effect is not equally secure under every boundary treatment.

Declared EVA compounds retain B positional effects while weakening A and changing thematic contrasts. Adding iin/in gives B +0.05392 [0.03011, 0.06811]; adding ee gives +0.05565 [0.03276, 0.07524]. Their A intervals include zero. These are comparisons of conventional encodings, not evidence that the collapsed strings are physical graphemes.

## Signed graphs, additivity and controls

Directional edges are signed target-minus-source position differences averaged equally over matched cores (at least two); occurrence-weighted estimates are saved separately. Thirty caption/document half-splits and frozen train/validation versus test comparisons test stability. Alternate prediction removes the direct training edge **and every one of its matched training cores**, preventing shared-form-mean leakage.

ZL B frequency-5 left edges have ten shared train/test relations, Pearson r≈0.755, Spearman≈0.212, same direction 90%, and within-line refit null p=0.06. The four alternate predictions instead give r≈−0.059, same direction 25%, null p=0.95. After inherited test-bifolio training purge, left alternate n=4 r≈0.225, p=0.75. Tiny dependent relation sets do not establish stable additive ordering.

Triangles use disjoint hashed core buckets for AB/BC/AC. Algebraic common-form-mean triangles are excluded. Six ZL B frequency-5 left triangles have median normalized closure≈1.068 versus null mean≈0.899, p=0.74. This fails additive confirmation. Some v101 right-edge relations predict strongly within that encoding, but the held-out convention mapping gate fails and ordinary-language graph predictability also occurs. Finnish frequency-8 left alternate n=6 r≈0.730; Latin n=24 r≈0.458. It is not a unique cipher signature.

Ordinary positional preference is a substantive competitor. On the same four ZL left held-out edges, alternate-path RMSE≈0.293 exceeds ordinary-preference RMSE≈0.208. Pooled ZL graph potential/preference correlations are ≈0.697 left and ≈0.936 right. These dependent descriptive comparisons do not identify a new state variable.

## Context and sequence limits

ZL wrappers worsen held-out context prediction by +0.518 bits/group. Latin improves by −0.0339 bits/group, including −0.0335 for the same-author Cicero comparison. Cicero work discrimination AUC≈0.918 [0.831, 0.977]. Ordinary morphology can produce both wrapper information and strong context separation.

Voynich same-hand context, literal ee/eee classifiers and primary same-hand HMM transfer lack enough independent held-out pages under declared minimums. They are non-estimable, not zero effects. Four/five-state all-hand Voynich sensitivities and Latin/Finnish controls preserve transition/emission adaptation gains and optimization warnings; none demonstrates a historical shared runtime. No random-label HMM refit null was run.

EVA vowel-designated deletion reduces byte-codec size, but versus approximately frequency-matched deletion controls its rank p=0.09; the worst matching discrepancy is about 22.5%. The ordinary-language deletion analogy uses the fixed five Latin codepoints a/e/i/o/u; it is not the complete Finnish or Turkish vowel inventory. Deleting information and compressing literal encodings cannot identify phonetic vowels or explain native handwriting.

Sequence tests use only consecutive original ranks, reset at unknown gaps, and test both physical x directions. A rank accumulator cannot add independent prediction when rank itself is in the baseline. Pixel running-state tests separately compare held-out caption MSE against rank/length, with bootstrap and shuffled-increment refits. The original merged model interval includes zero; the revised medium diagnostic likewise has a zero-crossing bootstrap interval despite an exploratory null p=0.03. Neither validates a manuscript-writing accumulator, especially after source failures and multiple specifications.

## Interpretation

The strongest replicated result is a convention-level B edge/rank association, with meaningful boundary sensitivity. The ground-up model has not shown that this signal belongs to validated manuscript writing units or measured writing position. Additivity, alternate-path generalization, alphabet equivalence and computational-state claims receive substantially weaker support than earlier chat summaries suggested. Every non-estimable result and failure is saved. Prior positional work is acknowledged in [the primary-source audit](04_prior_art_collisions_v1.md); no novelty, meanings or decipherment claim follows.
