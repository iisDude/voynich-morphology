# Manuscript-derived structural model and its limits

The deliverable is a neutral, source-traceable ensemble of geometric writing-unit hypotheses. It has not recovered a complete, validated sign inventory. The twelve-view pilot calibrated the method; candidate extraction, exact native masks and family discovery expanded across all 204 folio-captioned captures. These are capture views, not a certified count of physical pages or panels.

The original visual snapshot froze before comparison transcription contents opened. The handoff/chats had already disclosed conventional examples, so this was not complete researcher blinding. Its manifest hashes 1,512 artifacts. A later source-only repair tracks local writing density and transfers the original image prototypes without refitting. It is explicitly post-exposure and cannot be called a second blind confirmation. The original snapshot remains unchanged.

## What the model contains

Each source view has its capture hash, native coordinates, reversible PDF registration, approximate region coverage and caption-connected split. A candidate writing path contains an estimated baseline/body height and all original gray-gap group slots. Each group retains competing fine connected components, medium assemblies, coarser compounds and whole-group partitions. The group is not assumed to be a word; a connected component or prototype is not assumed to be a letter.

Native masks support measurable height, upper/lower extent, horizontal and vertical runs, raster holes, skeleton branches, repeated column ridges and threshold/connectivity sensitivity. All measurements refer to the assigned raster, not the writer's stroke sequence. Higher-resolution Yale captures improve inspection but represent the same photographed pages as the PDF; they are not independent evidence.

The original train-only family fits tested 32/64/128 bins; validation selected 128, the largest tested count. This is a quantization parameter, not a sign count. Source review of 128 fine and 128 medium families retains writing-consistent, variable, mixed and artifact categories. Twenty-one broad neutral visual structures and six proposed factorizations are separate hypotheses. They do not force all variants into one alphabet. The complete original atlas, with source examples, recurrence, variants and competing boundaries, is [the atlas](02_visual_unit_atlas_v0.md).

The source-derived model can be expressed as:

```text
photographed view → approximate regions → candidate paths
                                         ↓
                          gray-gap groups with original slots
                                         ↓
       fine components / medium assemblies / compounds / whole groups
                                         ↓
                 raw prototype / broad merge / factor hypotheses
```

Every arrow is an explicit hypothesis or transformation, not an inherited transcription boundary.

## Structural observations and rejected claims

Height is continuous under the provisional baseline/body scale. A two-component training mixture has centers around 0.819 and 1.461 body units. Its equal-caption validation log density improves only slightly over one or three components; three fit the test better, and were not selected retroactively. This does not establish two historical height classes or a discrete tall-sign inventory.

Tall uprights, loops and horizontal bars recur, including tall constructions connected to lower traces. Horizontal bars with end supports and above-bar ink are recorded as frame/insertion candidates. The frame proxy is sparse and unvalidated as a complete bench detector. Connectivity alone cannot decide whether a tall construction is atomic, a frame plus insertion, or a ligature. The six broad compound factorizations retain these alternatives.

Repeated ridges provide minim/repetition candidates. They are measured ink profiles and remain distinct from repeated prototype labels or literal EVA ee/eee. Raster endpoints, narrow skeleton necks and erosion changes do not establish pen lifts. All pen-lift and stroke-order fields remain unresolved.

The pre-exposure skeleton records propose internal cuts crossing at most two skeleton pixels. Those coordinates were later realized across all views while preserving the uncut alternative. All 211,121 resulting pieces conserve their assigned parent ink. Inspection of the twelve-view calibration examples showed that cuts often bisect visible loops/stems. The hypothesis fails as an automatic grapheme/pen-lift rule; the alternative geometry remains reproducible.

Conditional image-template reconstruction favored fine subdivisions over coarser partitions on 1,824 admitted test groups: mean IoU 0.621 fine, 0.538 medium, 0.458 compound and 0.415 whole-group. This explains selected binary shapes better; it is not lossless source compression or evidence that fine components are graphemes. Admission and source-mask selection bias the comparison.

## Source quality and coverage

The original snapshot has 3,935 candidate paths, 41,656 gray-gap groups and 280,910 fine raster components. Only 6,565 groups had completely admitted fine-family sequences. Unknown units retain their original positions and are not silently dropped from a row before rank calculation.

Object-presence audits found clear writing in 100/120 medium crops and 106/128 fine crops, with failures, ambiguity and eight incompatible broad assignments. These are not whole-row boundary accuracy or recall estimates. A later exact-mask audit corrected an earlier overstatement: neighboring ink visible in an RGB bounding crop is not automatically assigned ink. See [the correction](03_crosswalk_membership_correction_v1.md).

The tracked repair contains 3,272 candidate paths, 26,572 groups and 112,038 fine components, with 4,025 components flagged for substantial support in more than one row. There are 6,699 completely admitted fine-family group sequences. All four assigned partitions conserve the same group ink. The 32 prespecified entire-row reviews retain coherent writing bodies in some rows, but show substantial omissions, drawings and unresolved neighboring contacts. No complete writing-unit segmentation was certified. See [all source judgments](05_tracked_row_source_audit_v11.md).

Circular/radial writing has orientation-preserving path hypotheses in 17 views, with arbitrary angular seams and a 180-degree alternative. Sparse/rotated labels and separated flows are explicitly incomplete. They do not enter the horizontal structural assays. No claim of exhaustive handwriting annotation across the manuscript follows from processing every capture.

Nine pairs sharing a folio-side caption reference were inspected. No reusable writing-field alignment was accepted; distinct folded/extended configurations and fields occur under those references. Failed image matching cannot prove parchment regions disjoint. Physical panel boundaries, complete writing deduplication and physical bifolio reconstruction remain unresolved. Caption-connected views stay together in the original split; inherited bifolio labels are separate post-freeze sensitivity metadata.

## Contextual and hand stability

The fixed original prototype/gate model supplies 70,832 admitted fine instances and 4,230 caption/family measurements. Of 358 context/geometry contrasts, 312 are estimable under the declared minimums and 46 lack sufficient held-out captions. None passes the exploratory BH q<0.05 criterion. Broad A/B comparisons confound hand, section, layout and mask quality; lack of adjusted support is not proof of invariance.

Within-caption early/late comparisons keep normalized ordinal rank separate from normalized measured pixel x. There are 936 family/feature/coordinate records, only 126 meeting six paired test-caption blocks; the other 810 are descriptive. Prototype conditioning can itself constrain geometry. These tests neither establish allographs nor count historical scribes.

## Scientific conclusion

The model establishes traceable competing visual assemblies and exposes where automatic segmentation fails. It does not establish the manuscript's complete reusable writing-unit system. The primary positional assay has no qualifying minimal pairs under the original four admitted visual representations; this is a coverage/repetition failure, not evidence that the manuscript has no positional structure. Conventional agreement cannot repair the image evidence.

All four tracked representations also completed the structural, context, depth/repetition and HMM suites. Fine, merged and medium representations again have no qualifying primary minimal pairs; the factored representation has four across all contexts, including two in B. Those samples cannot support the registered primary regression. This is not a visual replication or a demonstrated absence of an effect. The second frozen snapshot preserves this post-exposure failure rather than redefining image units to reproduce a conventional result.

The decisive missing evidence is validated, complete writing membership and boundaries across ordinary rows, drawings, detached contacts, foldout panels, curved paths and sparse labels. Photographs also cannot resolve many pen-lift or stroke-order alternatives. These unknowns remain in the model and failure record rather than being replaced with EVA-defined units or a forced alphabet.
